## 🏥 Critical Care / Hospital EHR

| Dataset | Size | Year | Paper | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MIMIC-III** | ~40K ICU patients (BIDMC) | 2016 | [Sci. Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201635) | [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciii/) |
| **MIMIC-IV** | 300K+ admissions, BIDMC 2008–2019 | 2023 | [Sci. Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01899-x) | [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/) |
| **MIMIC-IV-ED** | ED visits 2011–19 | 2022 | (PhysioNet) | [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-ed/) |
| **MIMIC-IV-Note** | Discharge notes | 2023 | (PhysioNet) | [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-note/) |
| **eICU-CRD** | 200,859 stays, 208 US hospitals | 2018 | [Sci. Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata2018178) | [eICU](https://eicu-crd.mit.edu/) |
| **HiRID** | High-res ICU (Bern) | 2021 | [Sci. Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01535-8) | [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/hirid/) |
| **AmsterdamUMCdb** | 23K ICU stays | 2021 | (Crit. Care Med.) | [amsterdammedicaldatascience.nl](https://amsterdammedicaldatascience.nl/) |
| **PIC (Pediatric ICU)** | Chinese | 2020 | [Sci. Data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0510-y) | [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/picdb/) |
